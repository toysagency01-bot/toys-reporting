# BSCLINIC: confirmed counts and deferred leads

User clarification supersedes the initial annotation policy: use the first complete number for the main result, and keep bracketed values as deferred leads, excluded from patient counts, costs and conversions. Examples: `3(12)` → 3 patients + 12 deferred, `13(18)` → 13 + 18, `1{3}` → 1 + 3. A lone `[3]` in appointments is 3 appointments. Negative patient counts remain unresolved; `- (2)` supplies 2 deferred leads with no inferred primary patient count.

Added a separately editable deferred-lead count to BSCLINIC monthly results. The compact desktop view shows a note under the patient count; full columns expose the input, and mobile cards have a separate field. The period patient card shows the deferred total separately. These numbers never enter the patient denominator or conversion numerator. Original notation stays in source annotations and audit details include the new field.
